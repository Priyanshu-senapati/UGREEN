# Situations guide

This is a general guide, not legal or financial advice. Chaabi uses it to build a continuity plan from the family's own Family Map. The plan informs the family. It does not give anyone legal or financial authority over the owner's accounts, policies or property.

## How a plan is built

- Read the latest Family Map and show "Map last updated:" with its date and version at the top of the plan.
- Ask which of the three situations below applies. Do not assume.
- For each phase, include only the steps that match items actually in the map. Skip the others.
- Each step in the plan states what to do and why, the supporting document (NAS file and page), where the paper original reportedly is, whom to contact, the deadline, and the fact's status label from the map.
- Each step carries exactly one source label:
  - *Your policy says*: a quoted answer from the family's own document, found with `ug-document` `qa`, with its page.
  - *General guide suggests*: a step from this guide.
  - *Confirm with the institution*: the bank, insurer, fund house or pension office decides, so the family must ask them.
  - *Needs professional advice*: a lawyer, chartered accountant or similar professional should guide the step.
- End every plan with "Gaps to fill now", listing the map's open findings that the owner can still help resolve.

## Temporarily unavailable

Hospitalisation, temporary incapacity, or difficulty communicating for a while.

### First 24 hours
- Find the health insurance policy and the insurer's or TPA's cashless claim helpline in the map, and carry the policy copy to the hospital · *General guide suggests*
- Ask the hospital insurance desk to start a cashless claim using the health insurance policy number from the original document · *Confirm with the institution*
- Gather the identity documents the hospital asks for, using the locations recorded in the map · *General guide suggests*
- Tell the family contacts listed in the map who should know · *General guide suggests*

### First week
- List the payments due soon from the map (insurance premium, loan EMI, bills) so nothing lapses while the owner recovers · *General guide suggests*
- Check whether any bank account in the map is held jointly, because a joint holder may be able to operate it within the bank's rules · *Confirm with the institution*
- If a life insurance premium falls due, ask the insurer about the grace period and how to pay on the owner's behalf · *Confirm with the institution*
- Keep hospital bills and discharge papers together for the health insurance claim · *General guide suggests*

### First 30 days
- File any reimbursement claim with the health insurer within the deadline the policy states · *Confirm with the institution*
- Review fixed deposit and mutual fund dates in the map and note any maturity that falls in this period · *General guide suggests*
- Resolve the map's open findings that block these steps, such as a missing document · *General guide suggests*

### Later
- When the owner is able, review the map together and update anything that changed · *General guide suggests*
- Consider whether the owner wants to set up longer-term arrangements for decisions, such as a power of attorney · *Needs professional advice*

## Long-term memory or decision-making difficulty

Memory loss or a condition that affects decisions over a long period. Some actions may need legal authority, such as a power of attorney, that the family does not automatically have.

### First 24 hours
- Secure the original documents whose locations the map records, such as the bank locker and the property deed, without moving money or changing anything · *General guide suggests*
- Identify which family member normally helps the owner, and share the Family Map with them only through the NAS access the owner already set · *General guide suggests*

### First week
- List recurring payments from the map so essential bills, premiums and EMIs do not lapse · *General guide suggests*
- Ask each bank which arrangements exist for a customer who can no longer manage the account, such as joint holders or mandates · *Confirm with the institution*
- Ask whether a power of attorney already exists, and if not, what the owner can still sign while able · *Needs professional advice*

### First 30 days
- Check pension details in the map and ask the pension office how payments continue and whether a life certificate is due · *Confirm with the institution*
- Review insurance policies in the map and confirm with each insurer that premiums continue to be paid · *Confirm with the institution*
- Record the owner's own explanations in short voice notes while they can still give them, so the map gains context · *General guide suggests*

### Later
- Discuss guardianship or other formal arrangements if the owner can no longer decide for themselves · *Needs professional advice*
- Update the Family Map whenever documents change, and keep the previous versions · *General guide suggests*

## Passed away

The owner has died. This guide never prescribes legal procedure: succession, claims and transfers depend on the asset, the documents and the law that applies.

### First 24 hours
- Inform close family and the contacts the map lists · *General guide suggests*
- Keep the original documents whose locations the map records safe, and do not move money from the owner's accounts · *General guide suggests*

### First week
- Obtain several copies of the death certificate, because most institutions ask for one · *General guide suggests*
- For each life insurance policy in the map, ask the insurer about the claim process and the documents the nominee needs · *Confirm with the institution*
- For each bank account and fixed deposit in the map, ask the bank how a nominee or joint holder makes a claim · *Confirm with the institution*
- Where the map shows a nominee conflict or no nominee, get advice before making any claim · *Needs professional advice*

### First 30 days
- Ask the pension office whether a family pension applies to the surviving spouse and which forms it needs · *Confirm with the institution*
- For mutual fund and demat holdings in the map, ask the fund house or depository participant about transmission to the nominee · *Confirm with the institution*
- For a loan in the map, ask the lender about the outstanding amount and any loan insurance cover · *Confirm with the institution*
- For property, the bank locker and the vehicle, get advice on how ownership or access passes on · *Needs professional advice*

### Later
- Check whether a will exists, including outside the folders shared with Chaabi · *Needs professional advice*
- Update or archive the Family Map so the family keeps a record of what was done · *General guide suggests*

## Gaps to fill now

The plan ends by listing the map's open findings that are easier to resolve while the owner can still help. For example: a nominee conflict to clarify, a document mentioned in a voice note but not found, or a mutual fund with no nominee.
